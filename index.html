Signal guide

Triangle matches use a multiyear descending upper line. Short-base breakouts have a shorter flat floor and a current price above the prior eight-month high. Monthly key levels flag a close crossing a prior swing level in either direction, or moving within 3% of one. The early MACD watchlist tracks negative histograms rising toward zero. The 1-hour tab requires RSI at least 80 with positive MACD in the top 5% of the previous 180 readings, or RSI at most 20 with negative MACD in the bottom 5%. BTC weekly signals flag a fresh positive weekly MACD histogram or a weekly close at least 3% above the upper Bollinger Band. These are screening results, not buy recommendations. Open candles can change before close.
